UDL meets the ATR

Two frameworks, different purposes, shared commitments to effective teaching. Read, compare, organize, and check your understanding before our in-person ATR work.

Target: 35–55 minutes · Maximum: 60 minutesKnowledge and comprehension, not teaching decisions
Part 1 · Orientation

What are we comparing?

Universal Design for Learning

CAST's framework for designing learning to reduce barriers and support learner variability.

Engagement: the why of learning.

Representation: the what of learning.

Action & Expression: the how of learning.

Open CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 ↗

Aspiring Teacher Rubric

NIET's framework for describing developing teacher practice using 12 indicators and five performance levels.

Level 3 = Proficient; Level 1 = Improvement Needed; Level 5 = Exemplary.

The rubric addresses plans, assessment, instruction, environment, and professionalism.

Open the ATR PDF provided in Canvas.

These frameworks are not interchangeable. An overlap means they emphasize related practices, not that a UDL guideline earns a particular ATR score.

Find the shared language

Activity 3B · Match the shared emphasis

For each ATR indicator, select the UDL principle most directly connected to the description. Some ideas can connect to more than one principle; these questions focus on the emphasis stated.
