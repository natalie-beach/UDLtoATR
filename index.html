Part 1 · Start here

Different purposes, overlapping practices

Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

A framework for proactively designing learning to reduce barriers and support learner variability.

Three principles: Engagement, Representation, and Action & Expression.

Think: How do we design opportunities for learning?

Aspiring Teacher Rubric (ATR)

A NIET rubric with 12 performance indicators used to describe and evaluate developing teaching practice.

Each indicator has five performance levels, from 1 (Improvement Needed) to 5 (Exemplary); 3 is Proficient.

Think: What does effective teacher practice look like in plans and evidence?

The ATR is not a UDL scoring tool. They are not interchangeable, and not every ATR indicator has a direct UDL equivalent. The ATR includes planning, instruction, environment, and professionalism.
